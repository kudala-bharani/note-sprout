# NoteSprout demo plan

Status: script and shot plan only. No finished video or public demo URL exists yet.

## Purpose

Show how a friend studying computer science can revisit topics he has already studied. This is the difficulty he reported to Bharani; it is not a claim that he has used or benefited from the app. Keep his name out of the demo.

## Three-step walkthrough

1. **Revisit a topic.** Open the local app and load the included computer-science notes. Explain that these are public example notes, not the friend's private coursework. Click **Create study cards** with the real local model connected. If generation is shortened in editing, label the skipped wait; do not imply instantaneous generation.
2. **Recall, then reveal.** Show a real generated prompt. Type a short example response, click **Reveal source**, and compare the response with the original passage. Explain that the model generates prompts, not grades or an answer key. The written response stays in the browser tab.
3. **Return to what needs practice.** Complete the three cards with a mixture of **Got it** and **Practice again**. Show the self-marked confidence summary and **Revisit practice cards**. Close with the public source link and the local Node.js/Ollama setup requirement.

## Suggested on-screen copy

- “Built for a friend: revisiting topics he has already studied.”
- “Three recall prompts from your notes. Qwen3 runs locally through Ollama.”
- “Recall first. Reveal the original passage. Judge your own understanding.”
- “Got it is your assessment—not an AI grade.”
- “Revisit what needs another look.”
- “Prototype; feedback from the intended recipient is still pending.”
